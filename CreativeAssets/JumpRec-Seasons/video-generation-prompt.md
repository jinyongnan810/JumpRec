## Prompt

Create a polished 15-second Japanese anime animation from the four attached seasonal reference images. One cheerful adult woman continuously skips rope in the same park as spring becomes summer, autumn and winter. Match the references' cute character design, face, short brown hair, navy and turquoise palette, turquoise shoes and rope, soft cel shading and delicate illustrated scenery. Keep the character and accessories stylized, with simple recognizable shapes.

Use a completely fixed, slightly angled front camera. No rotation, zoom, pan, cuts or changes of viewpoint. Keep the same character identity, scale, position and park geometry throughout: path, cherry-tree trunks, fence, bridge, buildings, bench and lamp stay in the same locations. Show her entire body, feet, both handles and the entire rope arc, with room above the rope and below her feet.

Animate natural FORWARD rope skipping: from behind her body, up over her head, down in FRONT of her body, under both feet, then behind her again. Never reverse this direction. Use small rhythmic two-foot hops, soft landings, relaxed shoulders, elbows close to her sides and controlled wrist rotations. Both hands continuously hold their handles. The rope is one coherent continuous cord. Keep a steady rhythm through every seasonal transition, with subtle hair and clothing motion.

Follow this timeline:

- 0.00–3.75 seconds — SPRING: pink sakura blossoms and drifting petals. Lightweight navy long-sleeve athletic top and leggings. Small simplified smartwatch with a white strap visible near the cuff. No earphones.
- 3.75–7.50 seconds — SUMMER: the same cherry trees have lush green leaves in warm summer light. Sleeveless navy athletic top and navy running shorts with turquoise trim. The same white-strap smartwatch remains. No earphones.
- 7.50–11.25 seconds — AUTUMN: golden, orange and muted red cherry-tree foliage, with a few falling oval pointed leaves with finely serrated edges. Never maple-shaped leaves. Lightweight navy zip-up running jacket and leggings. The watch and strap disappear, leaving both wrists bare. Small white AirPods Pro 3-inspired earphones appear, with compact rounded in-ear bodies and SHORT stems ending near the earlobes. Keep them recognizable against dark hair, without oversized bodies or elongated stems.
- 11.25–15.00 seconds — WINTER: the same cherry trees are mostly bare, with snow resting on branches and gentle falling snow. Warm navy running jacket with a raised collar, thermal leggings and lightweight gloves. Short-stem white earphones remain; no watch or strap. Match the winter reference's soft breath puff close beside the viewer-right side of her face at mouth height. It gently appears and fades with breathing. Keep a rounded airy puff, with no visible jet, narrow trail or stream from the lips. Keep the face clear.

Coordinate changes in scenery, light, outfit and accessories with gentle transitions. Petals, leaves or snow briefly drift across the scene to soften each change. Keep the woman solid and recognizable, with no duplicated body, ghosting, anatomy changes or abrupt outfit popping. At the summer-to-autumn transition, remove the watch as the earphones appear; do not retain both devices together.

Use the closing moments for a brief soft transition from winter snow back into spring petals and the spring appearance, returning to the opening jump-cycle phase for a seamless loop. Keep the camera fixed and the rope moving throughout. No fade to black, titles, text, logos, watermarks, extra people or product close-ups. No dialogue or music is needed.

- Generate at the highest available native resolution.

If one generation cannot follow the full sequence reliably, generate seasonal clips using the matching references. Match the camera, character size, jump rhythm and rope phase, then edit them into the same 15-second timeline with short seasonal transitions.

## Review checks
