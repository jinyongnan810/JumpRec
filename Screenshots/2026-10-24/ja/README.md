Phone files: `01-ready.png`, `02-active.png`, `03-history.png`, `04-records.png`, `05-workout-details.png`, `06-complete.png`, `07-settings.png`.

Watch files: `01-ready.png`, `02-active.png`, `03-complete.png`, `04-settings.png`.

All PNGs retain native resolution and use RGB color without an alpha channel. The unused, fully opaque alpha channel was removed without changing color pixels or resizing the images.

Visible labels, dates, and record units use the Japanese localization. Active phone screens show headphone tracking. Watch toolbar buttons remain plain. Development-only controls and AI recap features are hidden.

## Review notes

- Duo captures show the outer display, including its system status area. Its bottom controls have the same clipping and overlap noted in the English set.
- The Japanese Watch count-goal detail fits without the truncation seen in the English capture.
- Chart samples are reconstructions from the reference screenshots, not recovered sensor data.
